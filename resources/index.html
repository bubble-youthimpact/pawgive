About us

We Pawgive and Pawget your mistakes.

Pawgive is a website that strives to help you find a companion to love, for shelters to have more visibility, and most importantly, find these animals a happy home.

Our belief

All pets deserve a safe, caring, and hospitable home.

Our most important role in adoption is helping you and shelters verify if you are ready for a pet. After all, adopting and taking care of a pet is not an easy responsibility to handle. We will help you determine if you are ready to take on a pet, as well as help you determine yourself if you’re ready through our manuals. If not, don’t worry, we’ll be happy to help you adopt when you are. Remember, pets are not disposable objects, or a simple transaction. They are a living being and companion to love.

Effective date: November 4, 2026시행일: 2026년 11월 4일

This Privacy Policy explains what information Pawgive (“we”, “us”) collects when you use our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. Pawgive is a student-run project that helps people find adoptable pets and animal shelters in South Korea.

1. Information we collect

  • Account information. When you sign up, we collect your name and email address. Your password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication; we never see or store it. If you continue with Google, we receive your name and email address from your Google account.
  • Saved pets. If you save pets to your favourites, we store the list of those pets with your account.
  • Foster listings. If you post a pet on the Foster page, we store the pet details and photos you add, your display name, and the contact details you choose to share (email, KakaoTalk ID or phone).
  • Contact messages. When you use the contact form, we receive your name, email address, what you are interested in, and your message.
  • Preferences. Your language choice is stored in your own browser, not on our servers.
  • Technical information. Our hosting and service providers automatically process basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the website and keep it secure.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and let you log in.
  • To save and show your favourite pets.
  • To review foster listings and show approved ones on the website. The contact details in a listing are shown only to logged-in members.
  • To reply to messages you send us.
  • To send account emails you ask for, such as password reset links.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

We do not sell your personal information, show advertising, or use your information for marketing without your permission.

3. Services we use

We share information only with the service providers that run parts of the website for us:

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore), for accounts, saved pets, and foster listings.
  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which delivers contact form messages to our email inbox.
  • GitHub Pages, which hosts the website.
  • Google Fonts and jsDelivr, which deliver fonts and code libraries to your browser.

These providers may process information on servers outside South Korea, including in the United States. Pet photos and shelter images may load from the shelters’ own websites.

4. How long we keep it

We keep your account information and saved pets until you delete your account or ask us to delete them. Foster listings are kept until you delete them or we remove them. Deleting a listing also deletes its photos and contact details. Contact messages are kept only as long as needed to respond to you, and normally no longer than one year.

5. Your rights

You can see and change your name on your profile page at any time. You may also ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to delete your account, by emailing pawgive@gmail.com. We will respond as soon as we can.

6. Children

If you are under 14, please ask a parent or guardian before creating an account or sending us your information.

7. Cookies and browser storage

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. Firebase stores your sign-in session in your browser so you stay logged in, and we store your language choice there too. You can clear these at any time in your browser settings.

8. Security

We use established services and access rules that let each person read and change only their own saved pets. No method of storing information online is completely secure, but we work to protect your information.

9. Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will post the new version on this page and change the effective date above.

10. Contact

Questions about your privacy? Email us at pawgive@gmail.com.

웹사이트는 “있는 그대로” 제공되며 어떠한 보증도 하지 않습니다. 법이 허용하는 범위에서, 포기브는 웹사이트 이용 또는 웹사이트 정보에 의존하여 발생한 손실이나 손해(입양 또는 파양에 관한 결정 포함)에 대해 책임지지 않습니다.

9. 약관의 변경

포기브는 약관을 수시로 변경할 수 있습니다. 변경 시 이 페이지에 새로운 약관을 게시하고 위의 시행일을 변경합니다. 변경 후에도 웹사이트를 계속 이용하면 변경된 약관에 동의하는 것으로 봅니다.

10. 준거법
